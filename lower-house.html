Lower House

Lower House is the newly renovated, contemporary venue on the ground floor of the Croatian Club Brisbane. Designed for flexibility and ease, it offers a modern backdrop for a wide range of events—ideal for celebrations, corporate functions, networking events, and casual gatherings.

Venue Overview

With its stylish black-and-white renovation, Lower House creates an inviting, light-filled space suitable for both formal and informal occasions. The open-plan interior is adaptable for banquet dinners, cocktail parties, presentations, and more. Large glass doors flood the room with natural light and connect guests to the outdoor patio, perfect for extending your event space or for alfresco dining options.

Lower House is operated as a private venue, distinct from the upstairs Lower House, ensuring exclusive use for your event and simple logistics for set-up and packdown.

Features and Capacity

  • Capacity: Up to 350 seated (banquet style), or 450 standing (cocktail style)
  • Floorplan: Open-plan layout with 50m² dedicated dance floor
  • Outdoor Integration: Direct access to a private, covered rear patio with open views over Mesic Fields
  • Bar: Modern service bar; options for beverage packages or pay-as-you-go arrangements
  • Kitchen: Fully equipped commercial kitchen supports both BYO and recommended caterers
  • Lighting: Ample daylight from floor-to-ceiling glass doors, adjustable lighting for evening events
  • Flexibility: Furniture, layout, and styling can be arranged to suit all event types

Event Suitability

Lower House is designed to handle high-capacity occasions with ease and efficiency:

The versatile space and indoor-outdoor design make it ideal for birthdays, engagements, family functions, and milestone events.

Lower House’s clean, modern aesthetic and flexible layout support seminars, workshops, product launches, and networking events.

The private patio and adaptable seating allow for club meetings, fundraisers, and casual get-togethers.

Practical Venue Information

Our venue is designed to adapt seamlessly to any event - from elegant celebrations to large community gatherings. Explore the features below to see how Lower House supports unforgettable occasions.

Neutral decor is easily themed or styled to match your event’s needs.

Exclusive booking ensures event privacy, separate from other club activities.

The covered patio is suitable for food trucks, BBQs, mingling, or outdoor ceremonies.

Lower House is accessible to all guests thanks to its lift, which is large enough to accommodate up to eight people. For large events, on-site parking is available for up to 300 vehicles, ensuring easy arrival and departure.

The interior is air-conditioned for comfort year-round.

Bar and Kitchen Facilities

The venue includes a contemporary, full-service bar managed by club staff to Queensland RSA standards. Packages can be tailored to your needs—choose from open bar, drinks on consumption, or custom selections to fit your guest list.

A large commercial kitchen adjoins the main space, available for external caterers or your chosen service, providing flexibility in food offerings. All kitchen amenities support high-volume meal service, giving you the freedom of BYO catering or working with one of our trusted suppliers.
